Before you write

Quick answers.

The things travellers ask us most — you might find yours here.

How long is the warranty?+
Every Montaro bag carries an 8-year hardware warranty covering wheels, handles, zips and locks against manufacturing defects. Register your bag after purchase to activate it.
Do you offer repairs after the warranty ends?+
Yes. Our studio repairs Montaro bags for life at cost — send us photos of the issue via the form above and we'll quote the fix and turnaround, usually five to seven days.
What's your returns policy?+
Try any bag at home for 30 days. If it isn't right, we'll arrange a free pickup and full refund — no questions, as long as tags are intact.
Can you handle bulk or corporate orders?+
Absolutely. We offer volume pricing, custom monogramming and co-branded luggage tags. Pick “Bulk / corporate order” in the form and our team will build you a quote.
Where do you ship?+
We ship across India with free delivery over ₹4,999, and to select international destinations. Delivery timelines and duties are shown at checkout.
